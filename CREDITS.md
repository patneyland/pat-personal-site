# Credits

## Character sprites

The body animation for Gary comes from **Stick Figure Character Sprites 2D** by
**Raphael Gonçalves** ([@rgs_dev](https://twitter.com/rgs_dev)), released under
**CC0**. Credit is not required by the licence; it is here because the work
deserves it.

The faces are Patrick's own drawings. `scripts/dev/make-pacer.mjs` redraws the
head clean on every frame and composites a face onto it, so what ships is a
derivative of both.
