An open benchmark for measuring how well AI does the real work of accounting,
so accountants can prepare for it instead of hiding from it. Extends
FinanceBench into role-based accounting tasks.

Pre-release, and the reason I keep saying the research question I went to a PhD
program to answer does not have data behind it yet. Building the measurement is
the part nobody did first.
